About Emily Chen

Meet your sound practitioner

Emily Chen — Singing Bowl Practitioner & Mindfulness Guide

Emily’s journey into sound therapy began with a search for stillness in her own life. That gentle curiosity blossomed into a devoted practice of creating soothing, immersive spaces for others through frequency, vibration, and mindful presence.

Using hand-crafted Himalayan Tibetan bowls (chanting bowls), she guides participants into deep meditative states where natural self-healing and total relaxation take place.

  • Certified Tibetan Singing Bowl Therapist (Trained in Kathmandu, Nepal)
  • Reiki Energy Healing Practitioner (Level II)
  • Dedicated Qigong & Breathwork Instructor

What To Expect

Your sound bath journey

Each session is intentionally paced so the body can soften, receive, and return with steadiness.

Gentle Consultation

We begin with a brief 15-minute consultation to talk about your current physical or emotional state and set your intention for the session.

Settling & Breathwork

You lie down comfortably on soft cushions with warm blankets while light guided breathwork prepares your mind to surrender to the sounds.

The Sound Bath Immersion

Surrender to layered melodies of Himalayan singing bowls, chanting bowls, and gentle tingshas echoing around and through you.

Grounding Integration

We softly close with moments of quiet reflection, herbal tea, and gentle grounding exercises before you return to your day.
